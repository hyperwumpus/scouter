# Scouter — guided by Glitch, by Hyper Wumpus

Scouter is the product. Glitch is its guide. Hyper Wumpus is the umbrella brand.

Business and Creator are separate modes serving different jobs. Recommend separate paid plans with an optional bundle after usage costs and willingness to pay are established. Do not require customers to pay for a mode they do not use. No billing or Creator analytics connection is implemented in this Business prototype.

## Business v1

Search by suggested business category or custom name, and town/neighborhood/postal code. ZIP queries are textual location hints, not strict postal-boundary filters. Review actual Google fields, public customer-path checks, and optional comparison with another supplied domain. Save notes and download the report.

Customer paths include contact, opening hours, website/listing phone consistency, social profile links, menu/ordering for food businesses, booking for relevant appointment businesses, and service inquiries. Returned links are not proof of completed checkout, message delivery or owner demand. Missing links in HTML are not proof they do not exist.

Services to discuss can include listing correction, phone/contact link repair, existing-social profile linking, menu/ordering path repair, booking path clarification, and consistency across confirmed official sites. Do not recommend a new site by default.

Further API candidates, not working features: owner-authorized Business Profile data for verified listing management; Search Console for a verified website; PageSpeed for specific performance evidence; search-provider integration for finding additional domains. Add one only when it answers a validated question. No new paid API integration was enabled.

## Creator later

Start with a creator's own authorized channel, an evidence-based report, one improvement experiment, and a follow-up. Add other platforms after checking data access and permissions. Asset creation can start as a service before becoming a built-in generator. Do not market a global creator rank or growth guarantee.
